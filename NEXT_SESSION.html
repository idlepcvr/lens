Then it stops being growth at all. At 150 BTC the plan is 5% nominal return, 4% withdrawal, 1% real — inflation plus living, nothing more. Not a target to beat.

Why this matters more than it looks. The Monte Carlo goes astronomical at P95 because it models one risk appetite forever. It doesn't know he stops. Phasing the model is what makes the tail believable, and a believable tail is what lets him hold a month-to-month structure without reaching for the account-blowing trade to close a gap that only exists in an unphased projection.
